---
title: "On learning in public"
date: "2026-08-28"
summary: "Why I keep notes out in the open."
tags: ["learning"]
---

Keeping notes in public forces a small amount of clarity that private notes
never demand. You write for a reader — even if that reader is only future you.

> The best way to learn is to teach.

So this is the plan: write things down, keep them simple, and revisit often.
